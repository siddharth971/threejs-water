THREEJS SEA Tropical Ocean

ThreeJS Sea

Open Tropical Ocean Simulation

Interactive Controls

  • Click + Drag Make ripples on the sea
  • Drag Orbit Rotate camera 360° around the ocean
  • Drag Object Move duck, sphere, or obstacles
  • Spacebar Pause & resume wave simulation
  • G Toggle gravity and buoyancy physics
  • L Align sunlight with camera view
  • Scroll Zoom camera in & out

Features & Optics

  • Raytraced Fresnel reflections & refractions
  • Tropical seabed with dynamic dancing caustics
  • Beer-Lambert water absorption & deep ocean gradient
  • 2D wave equation simulation & procedural ocean swells
  • Sun glint specular highlights on wave crests
  • Submerged obstacle buoyancy & fluid displacement
